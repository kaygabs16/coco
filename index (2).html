Our Story

Rooted in Nature,
Grown with Love

Nut of Coco was born from a deep respect for the coconut palm — the "tree of life" that has nourished West African communities for generations. We source directly from trusted local farmers, process with minimal intervention, and deliver products that are as close to nature as possible.

Farm to Shelf

Direct from our partner farms, no middlemen.

Lab Tested

Every batch tested for purity and quality.

Zero Waste

We use every part of the coconut. Nothing wasted.

Community First

Fair pay for farmers. Profits back into communities.
